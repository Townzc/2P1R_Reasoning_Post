the registered construction, proof, token, grouping and shallow-probe gates.
Independent archive verification also passed. This resolves implementation
feasibility for the proposed evidence-route task; it does **not** establish
LLM learnability, an allocation benefit, semantic-strategy diversity or novelty.

## Why this attempt and what it tested

The arithmetic attempts established that exact support/token/structure matching
could remove most of a candidate population. E010's primary, full-proof and
broader endpoints then gave different directions. Repeating those experiments
would not fix their population or semantic interpretation.

C014 instead tests a specified constructive population. Four disjoint chains
transport one of five states using consistent permutation tables; four
disconnected chains provide irrelevant evidence. Each available useful route
has four lookups. A separate solver reads exposed tables, and a strict proof
checker verifies cited facts and intermediate states. Intended path multiplicity
is guaranteed before sampling values, with no matching-based rejection.

Before execution, the attempt fixed100 semantic seeds,100 worlds each;
8,000 fitting worlds and2,000 audit worlds are separated by seed before
augmentation. The eight CPU predictors and five view tests were frozen, as
were the familywise rejection rule, token contract and conservative world
equivalence. CPU predictor fitting is distinct from LLM/SFT training.

## What matching still does not control

Both arms have3,232,000 supervised and36,384,000 processed tokens in the
accounting cycle, with128,000 supervised table lookups. However their realized
supervised distributions differ, as expected from allocating routes:

| Descriptor | Route-multi | Route-repeat |
|---|---:|---:|
| State-preserving transitions |25,599 |25,468 |
| Identity permutation tables |1,053 |932 |

The normalized total-variation distances are0.002172 for output states,
0.008563 for state transitions and0.021547 for permutation-table counts.
These are descriptive finite-assignment residuals, not significance tests.
Neutral exposure differs for7,041/8,000 questions and4,923/8,000 updates;
table histograms differ in6,000/8,000 updates. The round-zero update is shared,
which explains part of the exact equality in the remaining updates.

Do not respond by picking a more balanced assignment seed or filtering worlds.
The estimand remains the specified allocation procedure, including its changed
question-to-proof association and repetition/target multiplicity. It is not
an effect beyond every global computational feature. Exact token matching
resolves the old token-selection problem **for this population**, not all
possible controls or domains.

Useful and irrelevant deletions have equal full prompt lengths but different
realized positions. Their mean zero-based deleted-row positions are15.4600
and15.5275 across30,000 deleted facts each; the full position histograms are
retained. Their distributions are counterbalanced in expectation under the
frozen random renderer, not identically paired at every row. Deletion also
creates a real change in reachable evidence, which is the intended intervention.

## Interpretation and next decision

The useful progress is methodological: a constructive task can retain its
entire declared population while providing verified alternative evidence and
exact serialized supervision. Its scoped CPU tests find no predefined shallow
shortcut. This is stronger preparation than another small selected arithmetic
comparison; it is still preparatory evidence.

All routes implement one permutation-transport algorithm and share a bare
topology. New tables do not test topology OOD. The CPU probe audit set is now
observed and should remain a diagnostic sandbox, not be repackaged as an
untouched final test. Future scientific pools/splits must be fixed separately.
Passing this audit neither revises E010 nor establishes a positive effect.

The next sensible gate is a small, bounded A800 engineering check for the new
1137-token serialization: profile the unchanged1.5B full-parameter recipe and
test whether32 examples can be learned with valid complete proofs. Prepare
the runner, exact inputs and budget before requesting a server. Do not launch
the8,000-update accounting schedule or a scientific seed grid. The remaining
1460 GPU process-seconds have no new reservation; no GPU charge, server start,
LLM inference, storage expansion or old holdout access occurred in C014.
