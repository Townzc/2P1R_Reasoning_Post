# ICML / ACL 2027 research development

Date: 2026-09-11. Milestone LT001: **offline research design completed; experiments proposed, not authorized or run**. January 10 is an internal manuscript target. Venue choice follows the evidence and verified official dates.

The main question is: **At a common SFT dose and acquisition ceiling, does the observed cost of obtaining usable solutions change the preferred allocation between new problems and additional solutions, and does that change survive new problem pools and an allocation scale not used for selection?** The answer may be no. A cost equation, a P/K comparison, or a more complete log is insufficient as a contribution.

The closest overlap is the direct question/answer comparison in *Generalization in LLM Problem Solving: The Case of the Shortest Path*, including its MathQA extension. OpenMathInstruct-2, coverage-shrinkage work, repetition studies and iterative bootstrapping also constrain the claim. [Primary-source and author-code audit](CLOSEST_WORK.md) records exactly what was verified and what remains unavailable.

Three highest-value checks are (1) complete first-through-eighth-attempt yield and cost, including zero-yield parents; (2) three policies at an unseen allocation scale on independent pools under the same resource ceilings; and (3) a selection/verification sensitivity check that keeps the original problem denominator and distinguishes exact duplicates from semantic strategies. The third check starts with CPU records and blinded quality review; new training is conditional on a material, predeclared concern.

The primary student remains Qwen2.5-1.5B base, GSM8K, SFT only. The usable training/scoring release must come from the short-term study; it does not yet exist as a validated scientific recipe. MATH levels 1–3 and OLMo2-1B are conditional decisive replications, not parallel grids.
