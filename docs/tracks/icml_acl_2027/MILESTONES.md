## Three ICLR continuation cases

| ICLR outcome | Research continuity | Required distinction before another submission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not submitted | Continue the same research and accumulated negative/positive evidence; use the semester to finish validation | Improve the contribution and evidence; no need to pretend the two work streams are different papers |
| Submitted but not accepted | Preserve the submitted version and reviews; map each critique to a correction, test or narrowed claim | Confirm review/withdrawal status before another overlapping submission; disclose prior versions as required by the destination |
| Accepted | Finish course reporting with the accepted work acknowledged; retain reusable code and measurements | A later paper needs a substantive independent contribution, e.g. validated acquisition-policy behavior absent from the accepted claims. More seeds, one price formula, a renamed framing or modest extra benchmarks alone do not establish this |

While an ICLR paper is under review, use this track for analysis and planned extensions; do not submit a substantially overlapping manuscript elsewhere. The current ICLR policy prohibits overlapping archival conference submissions during review. [Dual-submission policy](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines). Future ICML/ACL rules must be checked when choosing a venue; no guarantee of permissibility is made from old rules.

A provisional venue-fit decision can be made after the results: an allocation/learning principle supported across settings may fit ICML; a carefully measured language-data acquisition and evaluation study may fit ACL. These are scientific planning judgments, not assurances of acceptance or verified 2027 scope requirements.
