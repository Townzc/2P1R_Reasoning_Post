# Closest-work audit — 2026-09-05 UTC

Scope: targeted reading of original-paper methods, reported results, and listed appendix sections; not an exhaustive literature review or reproduction. The research gap below is a hypothesis to test, not an established novelty claim.

| Paper and inspected sections | Direct overlap and experimental control | What our proposed comparison must add |
|---|---|---|
| [Data Repetition Beats Data Scaling in Long-CoT SFT, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11149v1), §§2–4, Appendix A and B captions | Trades nested unique-sample counts against epochs at fixed optimizer updates, batch size one. Uses Qwen3 and Olmo3 base models, response-only loss, BF16 and 8-bit Adam. Reports repetition benefits and memorization/termination diagnostics. Exact response-token equality is not established by its update-budget definition. The v1 negative-trajectory appendix captions appear inconsistent with the main table/discussion; numerical reuse requires checking the underlying results. | Repetition is a mandatory baseline. Holding updates alone is insufficient for our claim. Separate within-problem canonical structure, global structure-frequency coverage and surface variation, with measured supervised tokens. Track termination and train memorization alongside held-out correctness. Our short synthetic arithmetic task is a boundary study, not a reproduction of long-CoT results. |
| [CoScale-RL, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.14695v1), §§3, 4.1–4.3, 5.1–5.2 | Explicitly scales SFT solutions per problem and RL rollouts, with adaptive problem selection and re-distillation. Includes a single-solution breadth baseline and SFT solution-count ablations. Main pipeline uses Qwen2.5-0.5B Instruct; targeted experiments use 1.5B Instruct. This already overlaps strongly with a generic “more solutions per question” claim. | Our proposed contribution needs the narrower structural-diversity versus global-coverage/surface/repetition control in SFT alone. Generated solutions are not automatically different canonical structures. Its adaptive SFT+RL allocation does not isolate our proposed fixed-token, fixed-update estimand. Do not claim that solutions-per-problem scaling itself is new, or extrapolate our base-model smoke test to its Instruct/RL setting. |
| [What Do Learning Dynamics Reveal About Generalization in LLM Reasoning?, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.07681v1), §§3–5, Appendices A–C | Studies pre-memorization accuracy, robustness and data curation on GSM8K/MATH using Llama3 8B and Gemma2 9B. Half/quarter-data comparisons in Appendix A hold training steps fixed. Memorization thresholds are calibrated across runs; this is distinct from raw train correctness or NLL. | Preserve checkpoint-level raw train generations and reference likelihoods so memorization can be analyzed. A successful 32-example overfit is only a software gate. We have not implemented their pre-memorization metric; ordinary train accuracy must not be labeled as it. Any later threshold calibration must use development runs, with the final test sealed. |
